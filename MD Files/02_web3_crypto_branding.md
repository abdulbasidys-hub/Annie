# Skill: Web3 / Crypto Branding — Onety Method

## Purpose
This skill teaches the AI how to brand crypto, DeFi, memecoin, trading, launchpad, NFT, and onchain products in Onety's style.

The key rule:
**Do not design "a crypto brand." Design the specific mechanism, culture, or narrative of the project.**

---

## 1. Understand the Crypto Product Before Branding It

For every project, identify:

### Product layer
What is it actually?
- DEX
- launchpad
- AMM
- liquidity product
- memecoin
- index
- NFT ecosystem
- trading tool
- tokenized asset
- social protocol
- infra
- automation
- community project

### Mechanism
What makes it different?
Examples:
- absorbs large trades
- rising liquidity floor
- buyback/burn
- bonding
- yield distribution
- basket/index exposure
- no-fee design
- autonomous market making
- creator rewards

### User promise
What does the user get?
- less slippage
- more stability
- easier exposure
- stronger liquidity
- recurring yield
- simplified trading
- aligned incentives
- social participation

### Cultural layer
What kind of person will care?
- trench trader
- DeFi native
- NFT collector
- builder
- high-volume trader
- passive holder
- meme participant
- Robinhood-chain retail user

Brand from these facts, not from "Web3 aesthetics."

---

## 2. Avoid Generic Crypto Branding

Do not default to:
- neon blue/purple gradients
- coins floating in space
- network nodes
- blockchain cubes
- circuit lines
- futuristic HUDs
- holographic charts
- generic rocket imagery
- glowing Ethereum-like geometry
- random candlesticks

These visuals are allowed only when the mechanism genuinely calls for them.

---

## 3. Translate Mechanism Into Visual Language

Ask:
> If the protocol's mechanism were a physical law, what would it look like?

Examples of mechanism-to-visual translation:

### Absorbing price impact
Possible visuals:
- compression
- cushioning
- displaced edge
- mass entering a stable structure
- flexible boundary
- shock absorber
- material deformation

### Rising floor
Possible visuals:
- ascending baseline
- ratchet mechanism
- stepped foundation
- floor that locks upward
- progressive structural support

### Index / basket
Possible visuals:
- multiple assets collapsing into one object
- unified container
- one instrument containing many
- modular stack

### Bonding
Possible visuals:
- irreversible joining
- interlocked structure
- fused geometry
- permanent connection

This is the preferred reasoning style.

---

## 4. Crypto Copy Must Compress

Good crypto branding can often be summarized in one short phrase.

Examples of the type of line Onety responds to:
- "make room for size"
- "slip less, trade more"
- "only goes up"
- "the house always pays"
- "launch a token, bond it forever"
- "run your gym smarter" outside crypto, but same compression principle

A tagline should:
- express the behavior or benefit
- be short
- be memorable
- avoid startup jargon
- avoid explaining the entire protocol
- sound natural when spoken

Avoid:
- "revolutionizing decentralized liquidity"
- "empowering the future of finance"
- "unlocking next-generation capital efficiency"
- "seamless, secure, scalable"

If a tagline could belong to 500 protocols, it is useless.

---

## 5. Branding for Crypto Audiences

Crypto audiences are:
- fast
- skeptical
- highly visual
- meme-literate
- allergic to corporate over-explanation
- sensitive to status and momentum
- used to low-quality launches
- attracted to brands that feel "native"

The brand should feel like it understands the timeline.

Do not write like a bank.
Do not write like a 2021 NFT mint.
Do not write like a VC deck unless the audience is actually investors.

---

## 6. Logo Strategy for Web3 Projects

Good logo directions often emerge from:
- protocol mechanism
- ticker/name
- market behavior
- first letter transformed by mechanism
- one distinctive structural property

### Preferred
- simple silhouette
- meaningful geometry
- one strong trick
- recognizable favicon
- works in black and white

### Avoid
- token coin mockup as primary logo
- over-detailed chain iconography
- three unrelated symbols combined
- unexplained abstract starbursts
- generic "S" or "X" with gradient

A Web3 logo should look credible next to a trading pair, wallet icon, X avatar, DEX interface, and website header.

---

## 7. Banner Strategy

Default X banner:
**3:1**

Crypto banner purpose:
- establish world
- communicate mechanism
- give project personality
- support credibility
- make timeline profile memorable

Do not turn the banner into a litepaper.

Avoid:
- feature lists
- contract address unless specifically requested
- tokenomics paragraphs
- five badges/logos
- social handles
- unnecessary explanatory labels

One idea. One world. One visual system.

---

## 8. Project Narrative

Every Web3 brand should answer:
1. What does it do?
2. Why does that matter?
3. What makes the mechanism emotionally interesting?
4. What identity does the user get by joining?

For example:
A liquidity mechanism is not only technical.
It may represent:
- resilience
- depth
- stability
- size
- strength
- confidence

A tokenized basket is not only convenience.
It may represent:
- simplicity
- leverage of attention
- "one asset does the job"

The brand can use the emotional consequence of the mechanism.

---

## 9. Tokenomics Storytelling

When communicating tokenomics:
- find the flywheel
- show incentive alignment
- explain what creates demand
- explain what removes supply
- explain who benefits
- avoid fake complexity

Useful structure:
1. product earns
2. revenue flows somewhere
3. token or holders benefit
4. supply/demand dynamic changes
5. behavior reinforces the system

Do not use "number go up" claims as guarantees.

---

## 10. Launch Positioning

Before launch, determine:
- canonical project sentence
- one-line mechanism
- tagline
- visual hook
- profile image
- banner
- first announcement
- proof/receipts
- repeatable language community can reuse

Consistency matters.

If users cannot describe the project in one sentence, the positioning is not ready.

---

## 11. Tone

Preferred:
- confident
- concise
- internet-native
- subtle
- self-aware
- clever
- direct

Avoid:
- hard selling
- fake urgency
- corporate jargon
- emoji walls
- "We're thrilled to announce"
- "revolutionary"
- "game-changing"
- "next-gen"

Let mechanism and proof create excitement.

---

## 12. Crypto Brand Stress Test

Before approving:
- Does the logo still work as a tiny token icon?
- Does the project look native to the chain/community?
- Can a trader understand the product in seconds?
- Is the tagline memorable?
- Does the visual come from the mechanism?
- Is the brand distinct from generic DeFi?
- Could the timeline turn the idea into jokes/posts?
- Does the identity scale beyond launch week?

If not, keep working.

---

## 13. Signature Principle

> Crypto branding should make a mechanism feel like a movement without pretending the mechanism is magic.
